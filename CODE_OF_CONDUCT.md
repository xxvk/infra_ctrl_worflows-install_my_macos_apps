# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in macomrade to be respectful, constructive, accessible,
and safe for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and specific when giving feedback.
- Respect different technical choices and lived experiences.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Protect other people's privacy and security.
- Keep discussion focused on improving the project and its community.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, personal attacks, or publishing another person's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, contributions, reviews, project
communications, and public spaces where someone represents the project.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `xxvk@outlook.com` with the subject
`[macomrade conduct]`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with respect
for the reporter's privacy. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow
`SECURITY.md`.
