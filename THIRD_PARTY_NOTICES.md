credited in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

Before adding vendored code, copied documentation, media, fonts, models,
archives, or generated third-party artifacts, record the source, version,
license, required notices, modification status, and redistribution permission
here. The contribution must not merge until those obligations are verified.
